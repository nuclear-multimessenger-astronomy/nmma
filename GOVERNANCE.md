# NMMA Governance

## Branding authority

The NMMA marks are owned by Michael Coughlin, Tim Dietrich and Peter Tsun Ho Pang.
Any one of those owners may authorize NMMA branding or approve a
combined project name in writing. Requests should be sent to all three owners
at the addresses listed in `TRADEMARKS.md`.

Maintainer status alone does not authorize a person to grant trademark
permission. A maintainer may do so only if that person is an owner of the
marks or has been delegated authority in writing by an owner.

Peter Tsun Ho Pang owns copyright in the NMMA logo and authorizes its use as
described in `LICENSES/LicenseRef-NMMA-Logo.txt`. Logo permission requests
should be sent to Peter and copied to the other trademark owners.

Accepting a contribution does not transfer ownership of the NMMA marks to
the contributor or grant the contributor permission to use those marks.

## Licence changes

Code contributions are accepted under `GPL-3.0-only`. Any future change to
the source-code licence requires review of contributor rights and documented
authority or permission sufficient for the proposed change.
