Truthful statements such as "this service uses NMMA," scientific citations,
and links to NMMA are permitted. Such uses must not imply affiliation,
maintenance, endorsement, sponsorship, or official status.

Applicable nominative, referential, or other fair use under law remains
unaffected by this policy.

## Uses requiring permission

Prior written permission is required for NMMA branding in:

- product, service, project, collaboration, or organization names;
- domain names and social-media names;
- use or modification of the NMMA logo;
- combined branding such as "NMMA-Other Project";
- claims of official-service or maintainer status;
- merchandise or promotional materials; and
- branding forks or modified distributions as NMMA.

Independent services and forks must use distinct names, logos, and branding.
They may truthfully state that they use or are based on NMMA, provided that
the statement does not imply affiliation, maintenance, endorsement,
sponsorship, or official status.
